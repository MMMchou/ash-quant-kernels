# Head-to-head vs SGLang production kernels (2026-10-16, H200)

Script: `bench/bench_vs_sglang.py` (sglang 0.5.9, sgl_kernel 0.3.21, torch 2.9.1, triton 3.5.1).
Same AWQ tensors for everyone; Marlin goes through SGLang's own AWQ→Marlin repack and
`apply_awq_marlin_linear` (what `awq_marlin`, the default for AWQ checkpoints on Hopper, runs).
Raw table: `results/h200-vs-sglang-qwen3.5-9b.md`. CUDA-graph timing, Qwen3.5-9B shapes.

## Verdict: **Marlin is 1.67× faster than ashqk at M = 1** (and 2.6–2.8× at M ≥ 4)

All linear layers per token, us:

| M | fp16 cuBLAS | **awq_marlin** | sgl `awq` (dequant + mm) | ashqk | ashqk vs Marlin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3886 | **1990** | 9445 | 3325 | 0.60× |
| 2 | 3855 | 1998 | 9398 | 4527 | 0.44× |
| 4 | 3899 | 2002 | 9431 | 5580 | 0.36× |
| 16 | 3942 | 2225 | 9458 | 5863 | 0.38× |

Per layer at M=1 (us): in_proj_qkvz 12.7 vs 23.1, gate_up 23.8 vs 48.6, down 14.5 vs 21.5;
ashqk only ties on the small 4096×4096 projections (9.1 vs 9.2) and in_proj_ba (3.2 vs 3.3).

- Marlin streams in_proj_qkvz (25.2 MB int4) in 12.7 us ≈ **2.0 TB/s**; ashqk GEMV ≈ 1.1 TB/s.
- Marlin is ~flat from M=1 to M=16 (tensor cores, never compute-bound here); our GEMV is CUDA-core bound past M=2.
- ashqk **is 2.8× faster than SGLang's non-Marlin `awq` path** (dequantize to HBM + matmul), but
  that path is not used on Hopper by default, so it's not the bar.
- Accuracy: ashqk 2.0e-4 vs Marlin 2.7e-4 (fp16 cuBLAS 2.7e-4); Marlin 8–11e-4 on N=64.

## What this means
1. "Beats fp16 cuBLAS" was the wrong bar for W4A16 decode: the real production baseline is ~2× fp16.
2. An upstream PR / SGLang plugin replacing `awq_marlin` is not justified with today's kernel.
3. Why Marlin wins (to verify next): weights are **offline-permuted into tensor-core fragment order**,
   so a 128-bit load lands directly in MMA operand registers (no layout conversion — exactly the cost
   we measured in v3), int4→fp16 via `lop3` magic-number tricks (~2 instr / 2 values), cp.async
   multi-stage pipelining, and tensor cores even at M=1 (padding is free when memory-bound).
