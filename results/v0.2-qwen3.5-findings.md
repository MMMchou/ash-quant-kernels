## Notes
- `in_proj_ba` (N=64) has only 2 column tiles at BLOCK_N=32; added a skinny-N default
  (split-K 32) that took it from 5.9 us to 3.3 us. On CUDA, SGLang may fuse it into the
  qkvz GEMM (`finalize_fused_in_proj`) — to be checked for the quantized path.
- M ≥ 2 pattern unchanged from Qwen3-8B: only down_proj wins at M=2; M ≥ 4 falls back to v3 (~0.6–0.7× fp16).
- Fixed a flaky test: split-K fp32 atomics make results order-dependent at the ulp level
  (gemv: 164/200 calls not bitwise equal, max diff 4.9e-4), so the workspace-reset test now
  checks the error bound instead of bitwise equality.
